<h3>Recent case studies</h3>

In November 2026 I completed the British Airways and BCG X data science job simulations on Forage, then took both a step further.

[British Airways: lounge capacity and booking conversion](https://github.com/PatienceAnono/british-airways-analytics). I modelled minute by minute lounge occupancy at Heathrow Terminal 3 to work out how big each lounge needs to be on a busy day. The Club lounge needs room for about 340 people at peak. I also built a booking prediction model on 49,281 sessions. LightGBM reached an ROC AUC of 0.80, and the top 20% of scored customers held 51% of all bookers, which turns an unprofitable campaign into a profitable one. There's a small Streamlit app to play with both.

[BCG X: PowerCo SME churn and retention](https://github.com/PatienceAnono/PowerCo-SME-Customer-Churn-Retention-Intelligence). PowerCo believed small business customers were leaving because of price. After looking at 14,606 customers, I found price wasn't the main driver. Margin, consumption and tenure mattered much more. The churn model scored 0.73 ROC AUC on held out customers, and targeting offers with it would cut discount spend by 97% compared with discounting everyone.

<h3>Marketing analytics</h3>

[Marketing mix modelling](https://github.com/PatienceAnono/Marketing-Mix-Modelling-ROI-Analysis). Using 104 weeks of data covering $20.22M in revenue and $1.98M in spend, I built a regression model with adstock effects to see what each channel really contributes. The blended ROAS was 11.52. Moving budget from saturated paid search and paid social into email and influencer would add about $461,310 a year without spending more.

[Multi touch attribution](https://github.com/PatienceAnono/multi-touch-attribution-budget-optimisation). I compared five attribution models (last click, first click, linear, time decay and Shapley) across 11,193 touchpoints, 3,500 customer journeys and 1,454 conversions worth $229,294, to show how much the choice of model changes where budget goes.

[Email A/B testing](https://github.com/PatienceAnono/email-marketing-ab-testing-conversion-optimisation). A 90 day experiment on 25,000 customers. The new variant lifted the open rate from 23.99% to 36.66%, the click rate from 4.24% to 10.15% and the conversion rate from 0.59% to 1.76%. Every main result was significant at p < 0.0001.

[GA4 e-commerce funnel](https://github.com/PatienceAnono/ecommerce-funnel-analysis-ga4). I followed 270,154 users through the GA4 sample store in BigQuery. The biggest leak was at payment, where 40.8% of people who got that far didn't finish.

<h3>Customer and revenue analytics</h3>

[SaaS revenue health](https://github.com/PatienceAnono/saas-revenue-health-churn-intelligence-dashboard). Working across six related datasets with over 86,000 records, I tracked MRR growing from $1,832 to $416,815, found that enterprise accounts make up 65% of it, and flagged 463 customers with $114,087 of MRR at risk.

[Churn prediction and retention ROI](https://github.com/PatienceAnono/customer-retention-intelligence-churn-prediction). I compared four models on 5,630 e-commerce customers with a 24.7% churn rate. The final model reached an AUC of 0.81, and the retention plan built on it projects $503,848 saved from a $23,357 budget.

[Customer segmentation](https://github.com/PatienceAnono/ecommerce-customer-segmentation). For a beauty and wellness brand, I cleaned a messy CRM export of 1,000 customers, grouped them into six segments with RFM scoring and checked the result with K-Means. That surfaced 67 high value customers, averaging $799 in spend, who were close to leaving.

[Shopify cohort retention](https://github.com/PatienceAnono/ecommerce-cohort-retention-analysis). Across 15 monthly cohorts, 2,810 customers and $401,672 in revenue, about 62% of customers never came back after their first order, and only 9% were still buying a year later.
